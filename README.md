# id-eats

Three things built from one pipeline (modeled on `../wi-eats/` and `../co-eats/`, which are never modified):

1. **The web leaderboard**: every Idaho restaurant ranked on several boards (Top and Bottom), published as a private claude.ai Artifact
   from `site/` (generated by `scripts/make-leaderboard.py`). It uses the UCSD Google Local 2021 snapshot for ratings, labeled "2021"
   everywhere, so `site/` never goes in a public repo.
2. **Idaho Eats**, a free iPhone and iPad app (`IdahoEats/`, XcodeGen `project.yml`). No Google-derived data: places come from Overture
   Maps open data and official records; ratings and hours come live from Apple Maps' own place card.
3. **The website** in `docs/` (generated by `scripts/make-site.py`): landing page, guides, the James Beard honorees, city and district
   pages, how to look up an Idaho restaurant inspection, the web app at `/explore/`, privacy and terms. QA with `scripts/qa-site.py`.

See `CLAUDE.md` for the rules and commands, and `playbook/` for sources and calibration (`12-sources.md`), the naming round
(`05-naming.md`), the App Store listing (`06-app-store-listing.md`) and the App Review reply and recording script
(`13-app-review-reply.md`).

## Official records
- **Idaho State Police, Alcohol Beverage Control**: retail alcohol licenses, the one statewide official list of Idaho businesses
  ("serves alcohol", confirmation, licensed bars and restaurants the map data lacks). Exported by hand; licensee names never shown.
- **Inspections**: Idaho's seven public health districts publish results one search at a time (or not at all) and issue no grade, so
  there are none here. Each place links its district's lookup.
- **James Beard Foundation** honors 2023–26 and America's Classics, as facts. Idaho has no MICHELIN Guide.

## Hand-checked research
`data/research/` (private): James Beard honors, and regional lists of Basque kitchens, finger steaks and drive-ins, spud plates,
huckleberry, trout, taquerías, historic saloons and the oldest places, each with a 2025–26 source; closures with sources; leads that
couldn't be verified (`data/research/app/LEADS_*.md`); adult venues kept out of the app (`data/research/adult_venues.json`).
