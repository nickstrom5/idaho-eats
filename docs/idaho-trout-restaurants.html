Idaho trout, briefly

Idaho raises more farmed trout than any other state, most of it in the Magic Valley, where spring water from the Snake River Plain aquifer pours out of the canyon walls near Hagerman and Buhl. Rainbow trout from those farms, often sold as Idaho ruby red trout, is on menus across the state, along with smoked trout and, near Hagerman, farmed sturgeon.

Trout questions

Where does Idaho's trout come from?

Mostly from fish farms along the Snake River in the Magic Valley, fed by springs from the Snake River Plain aquifer. Where a place says where its trout comes from, the note under it says so.

What is Idaho ruby red trout?

A rainbow trout raised in Idaho whose flesh is red, like salmon's. Menus here list it by that name.

Is the trout on the list wild?

We don't assume. Most restaurant trout in Idaho is farmed; the menus and notes say what each place serves.
