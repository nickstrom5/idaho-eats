Boise, McCall and Mountain Home restaurants

On this site, Boise, McCall and Mountain Home is Ada, Boise, Elmore and Valley counties: the counties Central District Health looks after. They hold 1,315 restaurants, cafés, bars and bakeries across 18 towns, including 7 hand-checked Basque spots, 13 finger steak and drive-in spots, 4 spud plate spots, 3 places for huckleberry, 3 hand-checked taquerías, 4 historic saloons and supper clubs, 7 James Beard honorees and the oldest restaurants (back to 1957) from our hand-checked lists.

Health inspections in Boise, McCall and Mountain Home

Central District Health licenses and inspects restaurants in Ada, Boise, Elmore and Valley counties, about once a year. Like Idaho's other districts, it publishes no inspection results in bulk and issues no grade, placard or score, so Idaho Eats shows none.
