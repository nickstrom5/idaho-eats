Magic Valley and Wood River restaurants

On this site, Magic Valley and Wood River is Blaine, Camas, Cassia, Gooding, Jerome, Lincoln, Minidoka and Twin Falls counties: the counties South Central Public Health District looks after. They hold 461 restaurants, cafés, bars and bakeries across 32 towns, including 3 finger steak and drive-in spots, 2 spud plate spots, 3 places for huckleberry, 10 places for Idaho trout, 1 hand-checked taquería, 2 historic saloons and supper clubs, 1 James Beard honoree and the oldest restaurants (back to 1937) from our hand-checked lists.

Health inspections in Magic Valley and Wood River

South Central Public Health District licenses and inspects restaurants in Blaine, Camas, Cassia, Gooding, Jerome, Lincoln, Minidoka and Twin Falls counties, about once a year. Like Idaho's other districts, it publishes no inspection results in bulk and issues no grade, placard or score, so Idaho Eats shows none.
