Saloons, bars and supper clubs with decades behind them: 14 Idaho places still serving food, each read against its own site or a recent local news story in October 2026, with what its history rests on.

Old saloons and supper clubs

Mining camps, railroad towns and ranch country left Idaho with bars and dining rooms that have been pouring for generations. This list is old saloons, bars and supper clubs that are still serving, with what their history rests on.

How old, and where

Two of the 14 have a documented founding year at this address. The oldest with a documented year at this address: The Pioneer Saloon in Ketchum, 1950.

Where they are, by town: Ketchum (2), Stanley (2), McCall (2), Sandpoint (2), Wallace (1).

How this list was made: for every place we read a 2025 or 2026 source, usually its own menu page or a dated story in Idaho news, and matched the street address to it. A place whose only source was older, whose website had gone dark, or whose menu named the dish just in passing stayed off. Review sites played no part, and the places run by region and town, not by anybody's score. Menus change, so check before you go. Our sources are on the about Idaho Eats page.

Saloon questions

What makes a saloon historic here?

A long run that the place itself or a published source documents: a founding date, a building's history, a local honor. The note under each place says what we found, and a founding year is shown only when it's documented at this address.

Do these places serve food?

Each is on the list because it serves food as well as drinks, as its 2025 or 2026 menu or local news shows. Kitchens in small towns keep short or seasonal hours, so check before you go.

North Idaho

4 historic saloons and supper clubs, by town. The North Idaho page has the region's other lists.

  1. The Snake Pit

    1480 Coeur D'alene River Rd, Kingston

    HuckleberryHistoric saloon On the menu: Huckleberry Bread Pudding, Rocky Mountain oysters, Dante's Nachos, prime rib dinner · Season: Open Thursday through Sunday; closed Monday-Wednesday.

    Roadhouse also known as the Enaville Resort on the North Fork of the Coeur d'Alene River that has been a saloon, railroad stop, hotel and restaurant; Autumn and Viljo Basso bought it in 2024 and its current menu lists a huckleberry bread pudding.

  2. Tervan Tavern

    411 Cedar St, Sandpoint

    Historic saloon

    Downtown Sandpoint tavern known locally as the Tam O'Shanter or 'the Tam', which hosted a weekly open mic night in early 2026.

  3. The 219 Lounge

    219 N 1st Ave, Sandpoint

    Historic saloon

    Bar at 219 N. First Ave. that the Greater Sandpoint Chamber honored in June 2025 for its long history; owned by Mel and Claudia Dick since 2005, with karaoke and weekend live music on the Bonner County calendar in early 2026.

    219lounge.com

  4. 1313 Club & Saloon

    608 Bank St, Wallace

    Historic saloon

    Saloon and grill on Bank Street in downtown Wallace, now under new ownership (KimBerly Keyton and her mother Donna), with an upstairs club room for live music and a 2026 events calendar.

Boise, McCall and Mountain Home

4 historic saloons and supper clubs, by town. The Boise, McCall and Mountain Home page has the region's other lists.

  1. The Stagecoach Inn

    3132 W Chinden Blvd, Garden City

    Historic saloon On the menu: jumbo prawns, prime rib, top sirloin, loaded baked potato, cream pies

    Its own history says Schrier created finger steaks at the Torch, left the recipe with Milo (Bybee) and promised never to serve them at the Stagecoach, so jumbo prawns became its specialty instead.

  2. Diamond Lil's Steakhouse & Saloon

    407 Main St, Idaho City

    Finger steaksSpud plateHistoric saloon On the menu: finger steak dinner, finger steaks, loaded baked potato, ribeye, pork tenderloin chop · Season: Its site says it is open four days a week from Memorial Day to Labor Day; call for winter hours.

    Saloon, steakhouse and small museum at 407-409 Main Street in the gold-rush town of Idaho City whose menu lists a finger-steak dinner and loaded baked potatoes.

    diamondlils.net

  3. Foresters

    304 E Lake St, McCall

    Historic saloon On the menu: New York-style pizza

    Downtown McCall bar that its owners call McCall's historic downtown bar, now also serving New York-style pizza seven days a week.

    forestersmccall.com

  4. Lardo Grill & Saloon

    600 W Lake St, McCall

    Finger steaksHistoric saloon On the menu: finger steaks, burgers, loaded Lardo fries, spaghetti, hot buttered fudge cake · Since 1973

    Saloon at the west end of Lake Street on the site of the 1880s mining camp of Lardo, which The Takeout (2019) listed among Idaho places serving finger steaks.
